POPOPX Chat

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 14, 2026 Effective Date: August 14, 2026

Key Points

  • No account required — No phone number, email, or username needed
  • End-to-end encryption — Messages and files are encrypted before leaving your device
  • No message storage — Servers cannot read your messages
  • Minimal data collection — We collect only what's necessary to operate the service
  • Your data, your control — All chat history stored locally on your device

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

Account Information:

  • POPOPX Chat does not require any personal information to create an account
  • No phone number, email address, or username is collected
  • You may optionally create a local profile with a display name and profile picture (stored only on your device)

Payment Information (for subscriptions and token purchases):

  • Payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store
  • We receive confirmation of purchase but do not store credit card details
  • Device identifier (anonymous) for subscription management
  • Transaction records for purchased subscriptions and token packs

Support Requests:

  • If you contact us for support, we may collect the information you voluntarily provide
  • We recommend using in-app chat support to minimize personal data sharing

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

Technical Information:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version and configuration settings
  • Anonymous usage statistics (aggregate only, not linked to individuals)
  • Crash reports (if you enable crash reporting)

Network Information:

  • IP addresses (temporarily logged for security, deleted within 7 days)
  • Connection timestamps and duration
  • Server selection preferences

What We Do NOT Collect: Message content (end-to-end encrypted), contact lists, location data (unless explicitly shared), browsing history, advertising identifiers, or tracking data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Operate the service: Deliver messages and files between users
  • Process payments: Manage subscriptions and token purchases through Apple
  • Improve the app: Analyze aggregate usage patterns to enhance features
  • Provide support: Respond to your inquiries and troubleshoot issues
  • Ensure security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations: Respond to valid legal requests

We do NOT use your information for advertising, selling to third parties, building user profiles for commercial purposes, or tracking your activity across other apps or websites.

3. End-to-End Encryption

3.1 Message Encryption

All messages and files sent through POPOPX Chat are protected by end-to-end encryption using the Signal Protocol (double ratchet algorithm) with quantum-resistant key exchange.

  • Messages are encrypted on your device before transmission
  • Only the intended recipient can decrypt and read the message
  • Our servers cannot access message content
  • Even if servers are compromised, messages remain secure

3.2 Key Management

  • Encryption keys are generated and stored locally on your device
  • Part of the key exchange happens out-of-band for additional security
  • Keys are never transmitted to our servers in plaintext
  • You can verify encryption keys with your contacts through the app

3.3 Metadata Protection

  • IP address protection: Messages are routed through relay servers to hide sender IP from recipients
  • Fixed message sizes: All messages padded to 16KB to prevent size-based analysis
  • Separate queues: Different queues for direct and response messages
  • No user identifiers: No persistent IDs linking messages to users

4. Data Storage and Retention

4.1 On Your Device

Stored locally: All message history, contact information, profile data, encryption keys, and media files.

Your responsibility: Back up your data regularly. If you delete the app without backup, all data is permanently lost. Data cannot be recovered from our servers.

4.2 On Our Servers

Temporarily stored:

  • Encrypted messages awaiting delivery (deleted within 21 days)
  • Encrypted files awaiting download (deleted within 48 hours)
  • Connection links (deleted after first use)
  • Push notification tokens (for iOS notifications)

Not stored: Message content in readable form, user profiles, contact lists, communication patterns, or IP address logs (deleted within 7 days).

4.3 Data Deletion

You can delete your data by deleting individual messages or chats in the app, clearing all app data via device settings, or deleting the app entirely. To request immediate server-side deletion, contact chat@popopxchat.com.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Apple App Store

  • Payment processing for subscriptions and purchases
  • Push notification delivery (iOS)
  • App distribution and updates

Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

5.2 Server Operators

POPOPX Chat uses preset relay server operators to deliver messages. These operators cannot read your messages (end-to-end encrypted), do not create user accounts, do not store personal identifiers, and must comply with our privacy standards.

5.3 Analytics and Crash Reporting

Optional features that can be disabled in settings. We do NOT use Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, any advertising networks, or cross-app tracking.

6. Your Rights

6.1 GDPR (European Union)

If you are in the EU, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure (“right to be forgotten”), data portability, restriction of processing, objection to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time.

6.2 CCPA (California)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, opt-out of sale (we do not sell data), and non-discrimination.

6.3 PIPL (China)

If you are in China, you have the right to informed consent before data collection, access and copy of your personal information, correction of inaccurate data, deletion under certain circumstances, and explanation of data processing rules.

6.4 How to Exercise Your Rights

In-app tools: Settings → Privacy → Export Data / Delete Account

Contact us: chat@popopxchat.com — Response time: Within 30 days

7. Children's Privacy

POPOPX Chat is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or higher minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us immediately. We will delete such information upon discovery.

8. International Data Transfers

POPOPX Chat operates globally. Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United Kingdom, European Union, and United States. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers, including standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions where applicable, and technical measures (encryption).

9. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • End-to-end encryption for all messages (Signal Protocol)
  • Secure key exchange protocols (X3DH with Curve25519)
  • TLS 1.3 for all server communication
  • Certificate pinning to prevent man-in-the-middle attacks
  • Tor network support for IP address protection
  • Minimal data collection and retention
  • Access controls for server infrastructure

Your responsibilities:

  • Use strong device passcodes
  • Enable biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID)
  • Keep your device and app updated
  • Be cautious when sharing connection links
  • Verify encryption keys with important contacts

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we make material changes:

  • We will notify you via the app (if possible)
  • Updated policy will be posted on this page
  • Continued use constitutes acceptance of changes
  • You have 30 days to review and accept changes
