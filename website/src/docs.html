Documentation

This is the documentation corpus that ships with the POPOPX Chat fork. Almost all of it was written for SimpleX Chat by SimpleX Chat Ltd, the company whose project we forked. We publish their text unmodified, under the AGPL-3.0 grant that comes with it, and every one of those pages carries a notice above its contents saying who wrote it.

Two things follow from that, and they are why the notice exists rather than a rebrand:

  • The services these pages describe - relay servers, file transfer, the directory server, the business directory, notifications - are operated by SimpleX Chat Ltd. POPOPX does not run them, and our builds connect to the ones they operate.
  • Any address, store link, badge or contact inside those pages is upstream's, not ours.

POPOPX writes three pages of its own in this corpus: this index, the POPOPX trademark policy and the statement about the documents that are upstream's. Its statements about privacy are at /privacy/; SimpleX Chat Ltd's operator policy and conditions of use are published unmodified, under their own names, at /privacy/operators/. Where any of those disagree with a document listed below about who operates something, POPOPX's own text governs.

The lists below are generated from the pages this build produces, so they cannot drift from the corpus. Seven reference documents publish outside /docs/ - they are listed here at their real addresses.

Translations

Seven reference documents - the platform overview, the server, CLI, Android, contributing, translations and WebRTC guides - also exist in Czech, French and Polish, at /docs/lang/cs/, /docs/lang/fr/ and /docs/lang/pl/ with the same file names as their English pages.
